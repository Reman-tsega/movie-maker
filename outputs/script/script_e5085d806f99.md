# የጥንቸል እና የኤሊ ሩጫ — The Rabbit and the Tortoise: screenplay

*A fast, proud rabbit naps mid-race while a steady tortoise keeps walking and wins, showing children that discipline beats speed.*

**Audience:** Children aged about 5-12 in Ethiopian families, watching on a parent's phone (vertical) or a family TV/laptop (horizontal), usually with an adult nearby.

**Intent:** At the end, a child should be able to say the lesson in their own words: being fast is not enough, keeping going every day (ዲሲፕሊን እና ትጋት) is what wins.

**Voice:** gemini-3.8-flash-tts · Kore · language am-ET

## s01: Start of the race (wide, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** Establishes the place, the two characters and the race before anything happens.
- **Characters:** rabbit, tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [warm, calm storyteller, gentle start]
- **Narration (am):** በአንድ ጸሐያማ ቀን ጥንቸል እና ኤሊ የሩጫ ውድድር ጀመሩ።
- **Subtitle (en):** On a bright, sunny day, a rabbit and a tortoise began a race.
- **Back-translation (check):** On one sunny day, a rabbit and a tortoise began a running race.

## s02: The rabbit is fast (medium, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** Shows the rabbit's speed, the strength that will later make her proud.
- **Characters:** rabbit · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [lively and a little faster]
- **Narration (am):** ውድድሩ ሲጀምር ጥንቸል በፍጥነት ሮጠች፣
- **Subtitle (en):** As the race began, the rabbit ran very fast,
- **Back-translation (check):** When the race began, the rabbit ran quickly,

## s03: Looking back (wide, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** The gap between them is the setup for the rabbit's mistake; children must see how far behind the tortoise is.
- **Characters:** rabbit, tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [curious, then lightly amused]
- **Narration (am):** ወደ ኋላም ስትመለከት ኤሊ ገና በጣም ሩቅ ነበረች።
- **Subtitle (en):** and when she looked back, the tortoise was still far behind.
- **Back-translation (check):** And when she looked back, the tortoise was still very far away.

## s04: Pride (close-up, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** A close-up on her proud face makes the moment of overconfidence clear to a child.
- **Characters:** rabbit · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** (none)
- **Narration (am):** ጥንቸል በፍጥነቷ በመኩራራት «ጊዜ አለኝ» ብላ አሰበች።
- **Subtitle (en):** Proud of her speed, the rabbit thought, "I have plenty of time."
- **Back-translation (check):** The rabbit, boasting in her speed, thought "I have time."

## s05: The nap (medium, ≥ 6 s)
- **Why this shot:** The turning point of the story: the decision to rest becomes a deep sleep.
- **Characters:** rabbit · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [slower and sleepy, soft voice]
- **Narration (am):** ጥንቸል በአንድ ትልቅ ዛፍ ጥላ ስር ትንሽ ልረፍ ብላ ተኛች፤ ነገር ግን ጥልቅ እንቅልፍ ወሰዳት።
- **Subtitle (en):** She lay down under a big tree for a short rest, but she fell fast asleep.
- **Back-translation (check):** The rabbit lay down under the shade of a big tree saying "let me rest a bit"; but a deep sleep took her.

## s06: Steady steps (medium close-up, ≥ 6 s)
- **Why this shot:** The heart of the lesson: the tortoise keeps going, step by step, without stopping.
- **Characters:** tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [steady, even rhythm, calm determination]
- **Narration (am):** በዚህ ጊዜ ኤሊ ሳትቆም፣ ሳትቸኩል፣ በትጋት ወደፊት መጓዟን ቀጠለች።
- **Subtitle (en):** Meanwhile, the tortoise kept going, without stopping and without hurrying, with steady effort.
- **Back-translation (check):** At this time the tortoise, without stopping, without rushing, continued her forward journey with diligence.

## s07: Waking up too late (medium close-up, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** The consequence of the rabbit's choice; the lower sun shows time has passed.
- **Characters:** rabbit · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [sudden surprise, then worried]
- **Narration (am):** ጥንቸል ከእንቅልፏ ስትነቃ ጊዜው በጣም ረፍዶ ነበር።
- **Subtitle (en):** When the rabbit woke up, it was already very late.
- **Back-translation (check):** When the rabbit woke up from her sleep, it was very late.

## s08: Almost there (wide, ≥ 5 s)
- **Why this shot:** Shows the tortoise near the finish so the ending is clear before it happens.
- **Characters:** tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [hushed, building suspense]
- **Narration (am):** ኤሊ የመጨረሻው መስመር አጠገብ ደርሳ ነበር።
- **Subtitle (en):** The tortoise was already near the finish line.
- **Back-translation (check):** The tortoise had reached near the finish line.

## s09: Too late to win (wide, ≥ 6 s)
- **Why this shot:** The climax: the rabbit's speed comes too late, and the tortoise crosses first. The tortoise visibly happy at the win (user test 2: a 9-year-old asked for it).
- **Characters:** rabbit, tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [urgent, then warm and happy at the end]
- **Narration (am):** ጥንቸል በሙሉ ኃይሏ ብትሮጥም ቀድማ መድረስ አልቻለችም—ኤሊ ውድድሩን አሸነፈች።
- **Subtitle (en):** The rabbit ran with all her strength, but she could not get there first. The tortoise won the race.
- **Back-translation (check):** Even though the rabbit ran with all her power, she could not reach first—the tortoise won the race.

## s10: The lesson (medium, ≥ 8 s)
- **Why this shot:** Ends on calm and kindness, the two together, so the moral lands without shaming the rabbit.
- **Characters:** rabbit, tortoise · **Motion:** kenburns
- **Delivery:** [slow, warm and wise, gentle emphasis]
- **Narration (am):** ፍጥነት ብቻ በቂ አይደለም፤ ቀጣይነት ያለው ትጋት፣ ትዕግሥት እና ትኩረት ለስኬት ያበቃል።
- **Subtitle (en):** Speed alone is not enough. Steady effort, patience and focus lead to success.
- **Back-translation (check):** Speed alone is not enough; continuous diligence, patience, and focus lead to success.
